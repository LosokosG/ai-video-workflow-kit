# Recording prep

A checklist for presenter recording sessions, long videos and shorts alike. Both pipelines link it from their recording step; neither loads it as editing context. It comes from the author's own green-screen and phone sessions; the numbers belong to that example setup, so measure your own.

## 1. What does this session feed? (decide first)
- A long video only, a long video plus shorts cut from it, or native shorts.
- **Shorts will be cut from it:** tell the editor now. Keys for vertical crops must come from the full-resolution source: a 9:16 crop of a 1080p key is only ~608 px wide, too soft for a 1080×1920 short.
- On lines likely to become shorts, keep gestures close to the body; wide arm movements leave a vertical frame.

## 2. The day before
- `BRIEF.md` (or the short's script) has the chosen hook, the runner-up and the energy plan (`video-intro` → takes to record).
- Talking-point cards per section (see Framing for where they go). Numbers, names and commands that must be exact can go on screen in the edit.

## 3. Set
**Camera**
- The same capture profile every session: resolution, HDR on or off, frame rate locked, variable frame rate off. Write the settings into `RECORD.md`.
- No cinematic or portrait mode on a green screen: synthetic blur fights the key.
- Focus and exposure locked on the face.
- Nominal frame rates can lie (in the example setup, two "55 fps" shots were really 50), so measure real frame timing in the edit (`presenter-finishing`, stage 0).
- If LED lights flicker or band, match the mains: 50 Hz → 25/50 fps, 60 Hz → 30/60 fps.

**Light**
- On a green-screen set: a fixed, diffused key light; block window light (it drifts during a session and between takes, forcing the key and colour to be recalibrated); the screen evenly lit and wrinkle-free; sit far enough from the screen to limit green spill on the hair.
- Set any rim or hair light now: grading can't add it later.

**Sound**
- Same mic, same room, same placement; fans, AC and fridge off; the phone on do-not-disturb.
- Example setup: the room's noise floor was around −68 dBFS, so no denoising was needed, and the voice EQ belongs to that mic and room. A new mic or room: record a 30 s test and re-measure before the real session (`LESSONS.md` → voice processing).

**Framing**
- The bullet card right beside the lens, never below it: looking down at notes reads as a look-away.
- Head and hands in frame with margin for the key; the same distance and height every session.

## 4. Before the first real take
- 10 s of room tone with nobody talking (for clean joins).
- Green screen: 5–10 s of the empty screen (clean plate), then 5–10 s of moving hair and hands (a key calibration sample).
- A 10 s test clip played back on headphones: no clipping, no hum, focus and exposure locked.

## 5. While recording
- After a stumble, leave a beat of silence before restarting the sentence: it gives the edit a clean cut point.
- Say "keeper" after a take you liked and "again" before a restart; a transcript makes both searchable.

## 6. Take plan, in this order
1. **Hook:** the chosen variant, the runner-up (a different opener type), and the chosen one again with more energy (`video-intro`).
2. **Body,** section by section from the cards; repeat a section that stumbled.
3. **Outro or call to action** (long-form).
4. **Pickups:** re-record weak lines now, while light, mic and position are unchanged.
5. **Thumbnail and cover stills** (`video-packaging`).
6. Before tearing down, play back the last take with sound.

## 7. After (5 minutes)
- `RECORD.md`: capture settings, mic, light, anything unusual, the "keeper" takes.
