# Show the real result

`titles-thumbnails` · role: credibility · core: yes · stages: title, thumbnail · status: candidate · source: author-experience

**Pick:** Put only results on the thumbnail that the video really shows, and show them early

**What:** The package's hero element is the real output (the finished app, the measured number, the actual render): staged and designed, but never invented.

**Why it works:** A package that promises what the video can't show sends viewers away in the intro. A real result is both the reason to click and the proof that keeps them watching.

**How we apply it:** `video-packaging` step 0 lists what really happened and what proof exists before any draft is written; every draft names its hero asset, and `video-intro` puts that asset on screen first.

**Use when / skip when:** Always. If no real result exists yet, record one or re-angle the idea before packaging.

**Check:** For every element on the thumbnail you can point to the second of the video where it appears.

**Related:** [`retention/measure-the-hook`](../../retention/measure-the-hook/TACTIC.md)

**Source:** The kit author's packaging rules (`video-packaging` → Rules: "Stage the composition, never the result").
