# Keep every useful explanation

`retention` · core: yes · stages: structure, editing · status: candidate · source: author-experience

**Pick:** Cut stumbles and dead air, never useful explanations; no runtime target

**What:** Tighten the delivery (false starts, repeated words, look-aways, dead air inside sentences) but keep intros, outros, useful off-script explanations and deliberate pauses between ideas.

**Why it works:** On an earlier video, a forced one-minute cut removed the intro, the outro and useful off-script parts, and left jumpy cuts. Viewers who came to learn leave when the explanation they needed is gone.

**How we apply it:** No default runtime cap. Before a suggestion removes speech, `retention-review` transcribes the removed and the retained ranges and lists anything unique the cut would lose; the user decides.

**Use when / skip when:** All long-form videos. Shorts are the exception and cut much harder (`short-edit`).

**Check:** Every removal in a review lists what it loses, and nothing unique goes without the user's OK.

**Source:** The kit author's own edits ([`Workflow/LESSONS.md`](../../../LESSONS.md) → "Keep natural pacing and every useful explanation").
